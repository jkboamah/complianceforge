# AI Risk Classification Framework

## Purpose

Provide a structured method for classifying AI systems based on business impact, regulatory requirements, and risk exposure.

---

## Risk Tiers

| Tier | Description |
|--------|------------|
| Low | Limited business impact |
| Medium | Supports business processes |
| High | Influences operational decisions |
| Critical | Influences regulated or safety-critical decisions |

---

## Example Classification

| AI System | Risk Tier |
|------------|------------|
| Internal Knowledge Assistant | Low |
| Customer Service Chatbot | Medium |
| Fraud Detection Model | High |
| Credit Decision Engine | Critical |

---

## Assessment Criteria

- Data Sensitivity
- Regulatory Impact
- Automation Level
- Human Oversight
- Decision Criticality

---

## Governance Requirements

Higher-risk systems require:

- Formal Risk Assessment
- Executive Approval
- Monitoring Controls
- Periodic Review
