# Roles & Responsibilities (RACI)

## Purpose
Define ownership, accountability, and participation for GRC activities in the reference enterprise environment.

---

## RACI Matrix

| Activity | Security Governance | Risk Management | Compliance | IT Operations | DevOps | Legal |
|----------|---------------------|-----------------|------------|---------------|--------|-------|
| Policy creation & approval | R/A | C | C | I | I | C |
| Risk assessment (RCSA) | I | R/A | C | I | I | I |
| Control implementation | I | I | C | R | R | I |
| Audit evidence collection | I | C | R/A | I | I | I |
| Issue remediation | C | I | I | R | R | I |
| Vendor risk review | I | R/A | C | I | I | I |
| Incident response | C | I | C | R | R | I |

- **R** = Responsible (does the work)  
- **A** = Accountable (signs off)  
- **C** = Consulted (two‑way communication)  
- **I** = Informed (one‑way updates)
