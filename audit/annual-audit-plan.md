# Annual Audit Plan

## Objective

The annual audit program provides independent assurance that governance, risk management, compliance, and security controls are operating effectively.

---

## Audit Schedule

| Quarter | Audit Area | Owner |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Q1 | Access Management | Internal Audit |
| Q2 | Incident Response | Internal Audit |
| Q3 | Vendor Risk Management | Internal Audit |
| Q4 | AI Governance Program | Internal Audit |

---

## Audit Methodology

- Planning
- Evidence Collection
- Interviews
- Control Testing
- Reporting
- Remediation Tracking

---

## Deliverables

- Audit Report
- Findings Register
- Remediation Plan
- Executive Summary
