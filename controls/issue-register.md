## Severity Definitions

- **Critical** – Immediate action (< 24h)
- **High** – Remediate within 30 days
- **Medium** – Remediate within 90 days
- **Low** – Remediate within 6 months or accept

## Issue Workflow

1. Identified → 2. Logged → 3. Risk rated → 4. Remediation plan → 5. Closure verified → 6. Retired
